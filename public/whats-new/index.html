✅ Your three tweaks

☕ Mug moved closer

It now sits right by the keyboard. The coffee drains while you study, and tapping the mug makes them take a sip.

⟩ Collapsible side panel

On desktop, hide Shop / Goals / Stats with the ⟩ button (or press \) and the room grows to fill the screen. Bring the panel back with the tab on the right.

🔈 Separate sound switches

Music, keyboard typing, ambience and sound effects each have their own switch in Settings. Cars are much quieter and rarer.

🌍 A little living world

Pick one of five views out of the window. Each view has its own seasons, weather and day/night cycle, plus its own sounds and residents.

Random things happen while you study, and some can be caught for coins: tap a shooting star, a bird on the windowsill, a hot-air balloon or a UFO.

🎧 It sounds like where you are

Ambience matches the view: waves, gulls and a far-off foghorn by the sea; city hum and trains; breeze, sheep and a rooster at dawn in the countryside; pines, a stream, woodpeckers and owls in the forest. It’s all quiet, sits under the lo-fi music, and changes with the season and weather.

🎨 Make it yours

New users go through a short welcome: name your buddy, pick their look, then pick a room and view. You can change everything later, for free, with the ✏️ button on the room.

👆 Tap anything

Tap your buddy and they turn round, wave, give a thumbs-up or jump. They also chat on their own in speech bubbles, about the weather, the season, your streak, and anything happening outside. Pets and objects react when tapped too.

🔧 Under the hood

Tested

  • 168 automated tests
  • Real-browser checks on iPhone, iPhone SE, iPad and desktop sizes
  • A full QA playthrough and a separate code review
  • About 60 fps with everything bought; checked with the CPU slowed 4×

Sync hardened

  • No more endless back-and-forth syncing
  • Merges are deterministic: devices always agree
  • A new device won’t overwrite your buddy
  • Switching accounts never mixes data

Fair & safe

  • Catches pay at most 8 times a day
  • Manually logged time doesn’t count for quests or leaderboards
  • Leaderboard rows are private unless public; friend codes can’t be spoofed
  • Visit links are sanitised

📝 What I need from you

  1. Nothing for the basics. Everything is live at the app link above, and your existing hours and coins carry over. On your next visit you can open ✏️ to pick your buddy and room.
  2. For cloud sync + leaderboards: create a free Supabase project, open the SQL editor and run supabase/schema.sql from the repo (it’s safe to re-run). Then paste the project URL and anon key into Settings → Cloud sync and sign in on each device. The README has step-by-step instructions.
  3. Turn on leaderboards in the Friends tab, then share your friend code.
  4. Have a play in the Playground to see every view, item, event and character without studying 100 hours. Then tell me what you’d like changed!

Known rough edges

  • On a phone, visiting a friend’s room leaves empty space under it. On desktop, your own shop panel stays open while you visit.
  • Resetting data while the app is open in another tab can bring the data back from that tab.
  • The friendly ghost sometimes floats in front of your buddy’s head.
  • Room size is fixed. I made the room fully customisable instead (walls, floors, curtains, view and 40+ items).
